Mary Warrin affirmeth to ye Jury of Inquest, that she hath
been afficted by Giles Cory or his apparition, and hath
by hurting of me with his staffe, & pinching me
pinching & choaking me greviously tormenting thee & clitting me
with a knife, & particularly at yt time of his examination
he did greviously torment me: also at the time of his
examination I saw D. Cory or his appirition most sadly
afficted: Mary Walcott for pr. Inuent. Mary Lewis &
Sarah Viles Sept: 9: 1692

Eliz Woodwell uppon ye oath she formerly by her taken in
this Court, affirmeth to ye Jury of Inquest, that she saw
Giles Cory at meeting at Salem on a Lecture day, since
he has bin at prison he or his apparition came in &
sat in yt middlemost seat of our seats by ye post,
his worshipfull Day before, Bridget Bayly (his wife)
& I saw him come out, with yt rest of ye people, aff
Mary Walcott affirmeth of D. Cory as above, sitting in
same place at yt same time he or his Apparance, & he
she went out, wth rest of ye people: this she affirmeth
to ye Jury of Inquest Sept: 9: 1692

Eliz Hubbard to ye Jury of Inquest, that Giles Cory had severall
times afficted her, by severall sorts of torments, whereby
he is a witch & afficted me by witchcraft Sept: 7: 1692
