Mary Warin affirmd to y^e Jury of Inquest that she hath
been ~~suffer~~ afflicted by Giles Cory or his apparition and
that by beating of me with his staffe, & by biting me
pinching & choaking me greatly torturing me, & cutting me
with a knife. & perhicularly at y^e time of his examination
he did greviously torment me; also at the time of his
examination I saw G Cory or his apparition most dread
fully afflict Mary Walcot An Putnum Mercy lewis &
Sarah Vibber Sept^r 9 1692

Eliz Woodwell upon y^e oath she formerly has taken in
this Court: did affirm to y^e Jury of Inquest that she saw
Giles Cory at meeting at Salem on a lecture day. since
he has bin at prison he or his apparition; came in &
sat in y^e middlemost seat of y^emens seats by y^e post
this was ye lecture day before Bridget Bishop was hanged
and I saw him come out with y^e rest of y^e people. ~~at ye time~~
Mary Walcot affirmed yt she saw G Cory as above sitting in ye
same place at ye same time he or his appearance & if she
saw he him goe out with y^e rest of y^e people this she affirm
to y^e Jury of Inquest Sept^r 9: 1692

Eliz Hubbard to y^e Jury of Inquest that Giles Cory hath several
times afflicted me with several sorts of torments. I verily think
he is a wizard & afflicted me by witchcraft Sept^r 9: 1692
