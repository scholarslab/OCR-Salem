Mary Warren affirm's to y'e Jury of Inquest: that she hath been afflicted by Giles Cory or his appearance and that by hurting of me with his staffe & beating mee pinching & choaking me grievously tormenting me & cutting me with a knife, & particularly at y'e time of his examination he did grievously torment me: also at the time of his examination he said P. Cory or his appearance most fully afflict: Mary Walcott for p'sent. Mary Lewis & Sarah Vibber Sept 9: 1692

Eliz Woodwell uppon y'e oath she formerly by her taken in this Court, affirm to y'e Jury of Inquest that she saw Giles Cory at meeting at Salem on a Lecture day, since he has bin at prison he or his app'rion came satt in y'e middlest seat of y'e men's seats by y'e post his worshipfull Day before: Bridget Bishop (who was since and I saw him come out: with y'e rest of y'e people affirme Mary Walcott affirm'd y'e said P. Cory, or his app'rion at same place at y'e same time he or his app'rance did soe Runger out with y'e rest of y'e people: this she affirm's to y'e Jury of Inquest Sept 9: 1692

Eliz Hubbard to y'e Jury of Inquest said that Giles Cory had severall times afflict her & severall sorts of torments, first by pinch he is a wizzard & afflict me by witchcraft Sept 9: 1692
