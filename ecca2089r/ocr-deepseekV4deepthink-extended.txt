been afflicted by Giles Cory or his appearance and
that by hurting of me with his staffe: by biting me
pinching & choking me greatly torturing me & cutting me
with a knife. & particularly at ye time of his examination
he did grievously torment me: also at the time of his
examination he~~a~~p: Cory or his appearance most: did
