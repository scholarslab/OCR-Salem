Mary Warrin affirmd: to ye Jury of Inquest. that: she hath been: ~~[gtes]~~ afflicted by Giles Cory or his apparition and that by beating of me with his staffe: & by: biting me pinching & choaking me greatly tormenting me, & cutting me with a knife. & perticularly at ye time of his examination he did greavously torment me: also: at the time of his examination I saw: Sd Cory or his apparition most dredfully afflict: Mary Walcot Ann Putnam: mercy lewis & Sarah: Vibber Septr: 9. 1692

Eliz Woodwell upon ye oath she formerly has taken in this Court: did affirm to ye Jury of Inquest: that: she saw Giles Cory at meeting at Salem on a lecture day. since he has bin at prison he or his apparition: came in & Sat. in ye middlemost seat. of ye mens seats. by ye post This was lecture day before. Bridget Bishop was hanged and I saw him come out with ye rest of ye people. ~~at yt time~~ mary Walcot: affirmed. yt saw Sd Cory: as above. sit in ye same place at ye same time he or his Appearance & yt she ~~sat~~ saw him goe out: with ye rest of ye people: this she affirmd to ye Jury of Inquest: septr 9: 1692

Eliz Hubbard. to ye Jury of Inquest. that Giles Cory hath severall times afflicted ~~her~~ with severall sorts of torments. I verely think he is a wizard & afflicted me by witchcraft septr 9: 1692
