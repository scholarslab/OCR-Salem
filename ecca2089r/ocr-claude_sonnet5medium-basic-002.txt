Mary Warin, affirm'd to ye Jury of Inquest, that she hath been afflicted by Giles Cory or his apperition and that by beating of me with his staffe, e by biting mee pinching e choaking me greatly tortouring the e cutting me with a knife, e perticularly at ye time of his examination he did grievously torment me, also at the time of his examination, I saw sd Cory or his apperition most dredfully afflict Mary Walcot, An Putnam, Mercy lewis e Sarah Vibber Sept 9. 1692

Eliz Woodwell upon ye oath she formerly has taken in this Court, did affirm to ye Jury of Inquest, that she saw Giles Cory at meeting at Salem on a lecture day, since he has bin at prison he or his apperition, came in e sat, in ye middlemost seat, of ye mens seats, by ye post this was ye lecture day before Bridget Bishup was hanged, and I saw him come out with ye rest of ye people. Mary Walcot, affirmed, ye saw sd Cory, as above, sit in ye same place, at ye same time, he or his appearance e yt she se him goe out with ye rest of ye people, this she affirm'd to ye Jury of Inquest, Sept 9. 1692

Eliz Hubbard, to ye Jury of Inquest, that Giles Cory hath severall times afflicted with severall sorts of torments. I verily think he is a wizard e afflicted me by witchcraft Sept 9. 1692
