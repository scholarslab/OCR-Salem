Mary Warin, affirm's to y^e Jury of Inquest: that she hath been: ~~afflicted~~ afflicted by Giles Cory or his apparistion and that by beating of me with his staffe: & by biting mee pinching & choaking me greatly tortureing thee & cutting me with a knife. & perticularly at y^e time of his examination he did grievously torment me: also at the time of his examination. I saw: sd Cory or his apparistion most: dreadfully afflict: Mary Walcot An Putnam: Mercy Lewis & Sarah: Vibber Sept^r. 9. 1692

Eliz Woodwell upon y^e oath she formerly has taken in this Court: did affirm to y^e Jury of Inquest: that she saw Giles Cory at meeting at Salem on a lecture day. Since he has bin at prison he or his apparistion: came in & sat: in y^e middlemost seat: of y^e mens seats: by y^e post this was y^e lecture day before. Bridget Bishup was hanged and I saw him come out with y^e rest of y^e people: ~~[illegible strikethrough]~~ Mary Walcot: affirmed: y^t saw sd Cory: as above. sit in y^e same place at y^e same time he or his appearance & y^t she ~~[illegible strikethrough]~~ see him goe out: with y^e rest of y^e people: this she affirmd to y^e Jury of Inquest; Sept^r 9: 1692

Eliz Hubbard: to y^e Jury of Inquest: that Giles Cory hath severall times afflicted ~~[illegible strikethrough]~~ with severall sorts of torments. I verily think he is a wizard & afflicted me by witchcraft Sept^r 9: 1692
