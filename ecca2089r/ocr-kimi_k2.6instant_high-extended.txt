Mary Warin, affirmed: to ye Jury of Inquest:that: she hath
been: ~~often~~ afflicted by Giles Cory or his apparition: and
that by beating of me with his staffe: & by: biting me
pinching & choaking me greatly torturing me & cutting me
with a knife. & particularly at ye time of his examina-
tion he did grievously torment me: also: at the time of his
examination I saw: s'd Cory or his apparition most: dread-
fully afflect: Mary Walcot An Putnam: mercy lewis &
Sarah: Vibber Sept. 9: 1692

Eliz Woodwell upon ye oath she formerly has taken in
this Court: did affirm to ye Jury of Inquest: that: she saw
Giles Cory at meeting at Salem on a lecture day. Since
he has bin at prison he or his apparition: came in &
sat: in ye middlemost seat: of ye mens seats: by ye post:
this was: lecture Day before: Bridget Bishop was hang'd
and I saw him come out: with ye rest of ye people: & at ye same
time Mary Walcot: affirmed: I saw: s'd Cory: as above. sit in ye
same place at ye same time: he or his appearance: with ye
~~did~~ se him goe out: with ye rest of ye people: this she affirmed
to ye Jury of Inquest: Sept 9: 1692

Eliz Hubbard. to ye Jury of Inquest. that Giles Cory hath several
times afflicted ~~me with~~ several sorts of torments. I verily think
he's a wizard & afflicted me by witchcraft Sept 9: 1692
