Mary Warin, affirm'd to ye Jury of Inquest, that she hath been affli[c]ted by Giles Cory or his apperition, and that by beating of me with his staffe, e by biting mee pinching e choaking me greatly tortouring the e cutting me with a knife. e perticularly at ye time of his examination he did grievously torment me: also at the time of his examination. I saw: G Cory or his apperition most: dredfully afflict: Mary Walcot An Putnam Mercy lewis e Sarah: Vibber Septr. 9. 1692

Eliz Woodwell upon ye oath she formerly has taken in this Court: did affirm to ye Jury of Inquest, that she saw Giles Cory at meeting at Salem on a lecture day. Since he has bin at prison he or his apperition: came in e sat: in ye middlemost seat of ye mens seats: by ye post this was lecture day before: Bridget Bishup was hanged and I saw him come out with ye rest of ye people:
Mary Walcot: affirmed yt saw sd Cory: as above, sit in ye same place at ye same time he or his appearance e sd she him goe out: with ye rest of ye people: this she affirm'd to ye Jury of Inquest, Septr 9: 1692

Eliz Hubbard, to ye Jury of Inquest, that Giles Cory hath severall times afflicted with severall sorts of torments. I verily think he is a wizard e afflicted me by witchcraft Septr 9: 1692
