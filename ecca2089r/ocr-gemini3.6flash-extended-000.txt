Mary Warrin, affirm'd. to ye Jury of Inquest. that: She hath
been. ſote afflicted by Giles Cory or his apparition and
that by beating of me with his staffe: & by biting me
pinching & Choaking me greatly ~ ~ tormenting the & cutting me
with a knife. & perticularly at ye time of his examination
he did grevously torment me: also: at the time of his
examination. I saw. sd Cory or his apparition most dref
fully afflict: Mary Walcot Ann Putnam. mercy lewis &
Sarah: Vibber Septr. 9. 1692

Eliz Woodwell upon ye oath she formerly Has taken in
this Court: did affirm to ye Jury of Inquest: that she saw
Giles Cory at meeting at Salem on a lecture day. since
he has bin at prison he or his aparition: came in &
Sat. in ye middlemost seat. of ye mens seats. by ye post
this was yelecture day before. Bridget Bishop was hanged
and I saw him come out: with ye rest of ye people. at yt time
mary Walcot: affirmed: it saw sd Cory: as above. sit in ye
same place at ye same time he or his Appearance & yt she
for se him goe out: with ye rest of ye people: this she affirm'd
to ye Jury of Inquest. Septr 9: 1692

Eliz Hubbard. to ye Jury of Inquest. that Giles Cory hath Severall
times afflicted me with Severall sorts of torments. I verily think
he is a wizard & afflicted me by witchcraft Septr 9t: 1692
