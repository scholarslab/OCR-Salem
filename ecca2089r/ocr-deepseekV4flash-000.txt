Mary Warren affirme to ye Jury of Inquest: that she hath
been afflicted by Giles Cory or his appearance and
that by pricking of me with his staffe & biting me
pinching & choaking me grievously tormenting me & cutting me
with a knife. & particularly at y time of his examina-
tion he grievously torment me: also at the time of his
examination I saw D. Cory or his appearance most
fully afflict: Mary Warren for pr: hum: marry Lewis &
Sarah Vibens Sept 9: 1692

Eliz Woodwell upon ye oath she formerly by her taken in
this Court: affirme to ye Jury of Inquest, that she saw
Giles Cory at meeting at Salem on a lecture day, since
he has bin at prison he or his appearance, came in to
ye seat, in ye meeting seat, of ye new seats by ye post
his worshipture Day before: Bridget Bishop (who was hang)
and I saw him come out with y rest of ye people: and
mary Walcot: affirme yt she saw D. Cory at the above said
same place at y same time he or his appearance & she
did Ring out with y rest of ye people: this she affirm
to ye Jury of Inquest Sept 9: 1692

Eliza Hubbard to ye Jury of Inquest yt Giles Cory had severall
times afflicted her, & severall sorts of torments, Fearly by pinch
he is a wizard & afflicted me by witchcraft Sept 9: 1692
