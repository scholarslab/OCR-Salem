Mary Maria Affina: to y Funs of Frugal: that the said  
seen: for off the by Giles Cory or his appearance an  
that by beating of me with his staff: by biting me  
pinching or choking me greatly tormenting me: & calling me  
with a knife. & particularly at y time of the examination  
& of grievously torment me: also at the time of his  
examination: & saw: Cory or his appearance most: &  
fully afflict: many times in & woman: merry tears: &  
Sarah: Bibber Sept. 9. 1692  

Eliz. Howard upon her oath the former by her taken in  
his court: & affirm by y Funs of Frugal: that she saw  
Giles Cory at meeting at Salem on a lecture day. Since  
he has bin at prison he or his appearance: came in  
sat: in y most east seat: of ye men's seats: by the  
his daughter the day before: Bridget Brigham say change  
and she saw him come and afflict y rank of y people: &  
Mary Walcot: affirm: that Cory: & above: sit in y  
same place at y same time: he or his appearance: & if the  
same thing go out: with y rank of y people: this they affirm  
to y Funs of Frugal: Sept. 9. 1692  

Eliz. Howard: to y Funs of Frugal: that Giles Cory fell several  
him off all: & be still several sort of torment: & very likely  
he is a wizard: & afflicted me by unknown Sept. 9. 1692
