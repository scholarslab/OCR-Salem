Mary Waren affirmd: to yᵉ Jury of Inquest: that: she hath been: ~~have~~ afflicted by Giles Cory or his apperisition and that by beating of me with his staffe: & by: biting mee pinching & choaking me greatly tortureing me & cutting me with a knife. & perticulerly at yᵉ time of his examination he did greivously torment me: also: at the time of his examination. I saw: sᵈ Cory or his appearition most: dredfully afflict: Mary Walcot An Putnam: mercy lewis & Sarah: Vibber &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Septᵉ. 9. 1692

Eliz Woodwell upon yᵉ oath she formerly has taken in this Court: did affirm to yᵉ Jury of Inquest: that: she saw Giles Cory at meeting at Salem on a lecture day. since he has bin at prison he or his aperition: came in & sat: in yᵉ middlemost seat. of yᵉ mens seats: by yᵉ post this was lecture day before. Bridget Bishup was hanged and I saw him come out: with yᵉ rest of yᵉ people. ~~at yᵉ time~~ mary Walcot: affirmed: yᵗ ~~she saw~~ sᵈ Cory: as above. sit in yᵉ same place at yᵉ same time he or his Appearance & yᵗ she ~~saw~~ se him goe out: with yᵉ rest of yᵉ people: this she affirmd to yᵉ Jury of Inquest: Septᵉ 9. 1692
