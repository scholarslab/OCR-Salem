Mary Maria Afflicted to ye Funs of Frugal: that the said  
Giles Cory or his appearance an  
that by beating of me with his staff: by biting me  
pinching or choaking me greatly tormenting me: & calling me  
with a knife. & particularly at ye time of this examination  
& of grievously torment me: also at the time of his  
examination: & saw: Cory or his appearance most: &  
fully afflict: many times in & woman: mercy tears: &  
Sarah: Vibber Sept. 9. 1692  

Eliz. Howard upon her oath the former by her taken in  
his court: & affirm by ye Funs of Frugal: that she saw  
giles Cory at meeting at Salem on a lecture day. Since  
he has been at prison he or his appearance: came in  
sat: in ye mosteast seat: of women seats: by pop  
his daughter Say before: Bridget Brigham say change  
and F saw him come and invite my rank of people: &  
that Ray Walcot: affirm: that & Cory: & above: sit in  
same place at ye same time: he or his appearance: & if the  
same Kinggo out: will: my right of people: this they affirm  
to ye Funs of Frugal: Sept. 9. 1692  

Eliz. Howard: to ye Funs of Frugal: that Giles Cory had Severed  
him off: & brought several son of to man: & very likely  
he's a wizard: & afflict me by unknaft Sept. 9. 1692
