Mary Warin, affirmed: to ye Jury of Inquest: that: she hath been: afflicted by Giles Cory or his apparition and that by beating of me with his staff: & by: biting me pinching & choaking me greatly torturing me & cutting me with a knife. & particularly at ye time of his examination he did grievously torment me: also: at the time of his examination I saw: D Cory or his apparition most: dreadfully afflict: Mary Walcot An Putnam: mercy lewis & Sarah: Vibber Sept. 9: 1692

Eliz Woodwell upon ye oath she formerly has taken in this Court: did affirm to ye Jury of Inquest: that: she saw Giles Cory at meeting at Salem on a lecture day. Since he has been at prison he or his apparition: came in & sat: in ye middlemost seat: of ye mens seats: by ye post this was lecture Day before Bridget Bishop was hanged and I saw him come out with ye rest of ye people. Mary Walcot: affirmed: I saw D Cory: as above. sit in ye same place at ye same time he or his appearance & it was she Ringoe out: with ye rest of ye people: this she affirmed to ye Jury of Inquest: Septr 9: 1692

Eliz Hubbard: to ye Jury of Inquest: that Giles Cory hath several times afflicted me with several sorts of torments. I verily think he's a wizard & afflicted me by witchcraft Septr 9: 1692
