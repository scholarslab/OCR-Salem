Mary Walcott affirms to the Court: that the said Giles Corey, or his appearance, did by beating of me with his staff, by biting me, pinching or choking me, greatly torment me, and threatening me with a knife; particularly at the time of the examination, and did grievously torment me; also at the time of his examination, I saw Corey or his appearance most fully afflict many times ... and Sarah Bibber, Sept. 9, 1692.

Elizabeth Howard, upon my call, the former deposition taken in his court, and affirm by the Court, that she saw Giles Corey at meeting at Salem on a lecture day. Since he has been in prison, he or his appearance came in and sat in the north-east seat of the men's ... by ... his daughter ... Bridget Brigham say ... and she saw him come and afflict the rank of the people; and Mary Walcott affirms that she saw Corey sit in the same place at the same time, he or his appearance, and ... this they affirm to the Court. Sept. 9, 1692.
