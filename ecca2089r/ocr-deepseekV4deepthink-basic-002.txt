Mary Maria Affina: to y Funs of Frugal: that the fall  
been: for off the by Silas Cory or his appearance an  
that by beating of me with his flaffe: by biting me  
pincing or choaking me greatly tormenting me: & calling me  
with a knife. & perchearly at y time of the examination  
& of grievously torment me: also at the time of his  
examination: & saw: Cory or his appearance most: &  
fully afflict: many times in & woman: merry tears: &  
Sarah: Nibber Sept. 9. 1692  

Eliz. Howard upon my call the former by her taken in  
his court: & affirm by y Funs of Frugal: that the saw  
giles Cory at meeting at Salem on a lecture day. Since  
he has bin at prison he or his appearance: came in  
sat: in y mosteast seat: of ye men feels: by pop  
his daughter Day before: Bridget Brigham say change  
and F saw him come and invite y rank of y people: &  
tharay Walcot: affirm: that & Cory: & above: sit in y  
same place at y same time: he or his appearance: & if the  
same Kinggo out: will y rank of y people: this the affim  
to y Funs of Frugal: Sept. 9. 1692  

Eliz. Howard: to y Funs of Frugal: that Silas Cory fall severall  
him off all: & be still several sort of torment: & very likely  
he is a wizen: & aff the me by unknaft Sept. 9. 1692
