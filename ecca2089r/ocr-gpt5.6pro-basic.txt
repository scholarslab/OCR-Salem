Mary Warin, affirmed: to yᵉ Jury of Inquest: that: she hath
been: ~~formerly~~ afflicted by Giles Cory or his apparition and
that by beating of mee with his staffe: & by biting mee &
pinching & choaking mee greatly torturing mee & cutting mee
with a knife. & particularly at yᵉ time of his examination how
he did grievously torment mee: also: at the time of his
examination. I saw: sᵈ Cory or his apparition most: dread
fully afflict: Mary Walcot Ann Putnam: Mercy Lewis &
Sarah: Vibber Septʳ. 9. 1692

Eliz Woodwell upon yᵉ oath she formerly has taken in
this court: did affirm to yᵉ Jury of Inquest: that: she saw
Giles Cory at meeting at Salem on a lecture day. Since
he has bin at prison he or his apparition: came in &
sat: in yᵉ middlemost seat: of yᵉ mens seats: by yᵉ post
this was yᵉ lecture day Before: Bridget Bishop was hanged
and I saw him come out: with yᵉ rest of yᵉ people: ~~& further~~
Mary Walcot: affirmed: yᵗ she saw sᵈ Cory: as above: sit in yᵉ
same place at yᵉ same time: he or his Appearance & yᵗ she
did se him goe out: with yᵉ rest of yᵉ people: this she affirmd
to yᵉ Jury of Inquest: Septʳ. 9. 1692

Eliz Hubbard: to yᵉ Jury of Inquest: that Giles Cory hath several
times afflicted me with several sorts of torments: & verily thinks
he is a wizard & afflicted me by witchcraft Septʳ. 9. 1692
