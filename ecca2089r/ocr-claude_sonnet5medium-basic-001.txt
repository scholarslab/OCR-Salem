Eliz Woodwell upon y^e oath she formerly has taken in
this Court: did affirm to y^e Jury of Inquest. that she saw
Giles Cory at meeting at Salem on a lecture day. Since
he has bin at prison he or his aparition. came in e
sat: in y^e middlemost seat: of y^e mens seats. by y^e post
this was lecture day before. Bridget Bishup was hanged
and I saw him come out. with y^e rest of y^e people. [struck through]
Mary Walcot: affirmed. y^t saw sd Cory: as above. sit in y^e
same place at y^e same time he or his appearance e y^t she
did se him goe out: with y^e rest of y^e people: this the affirm'd
to y^e Jury of Inquest: Sept^r 9: 1692


Eliz Hubbard. to y^e Jury of Inquest. that Giles Cory hath several
times afflicted with several sorts of torments. I verily think
he is a wizard e afflicted me by witchcraft Sept^r 9: 1692
