Mary Warin affirmd: to yᵉ Jury of Inquest: that: she hath
been: ~~been~~ afflicted by Giles Cory or his apperisition and
that by beating of me with his staffe: & by biting mee
pinching & choaking me greatly tortureing me & cutting me
with a knife. & perticulerly at yᵉ time of his examination
he did greivously torment me: also: at the time of his
examination I saw: sd Cory or his appearition most: dred
fully afflict: Mary Walcot An Putnam: mercy lewis &
Sarah: Vibber      Septᵉ 9. 1692

Eliz Woodwell upon yᵉ oath she formerly has taken in
this Court: did affirm to yᵉ Jury of Inquest: that: she saw
Giles Cory at meeting at Salem on a lecture day. since
he has bin at prison he or his aperition: came in &
sat: in yᵉ middlemost seat. of yᵉ mens seats: by yᵉ post
this was lecture day before: Bridget Bishup was hanged
and I saw him come out: with yᵉ rest of yᵉ people. ~~at yᵗ time~~
mary Walcot: affirmed: yᵗ shee saw sd Cory: as above. sit in yᵉ
same place at yᵉ same time he or his Appearance & yᵗ she
~~saw~~ se him goe out: with yᵉ rest of yᵉ people: this she affirmd
to yᵉ Jury of Inquest: Septᵉ 9. 1692

Eliz Hubbard: to yᵉ Jury of Inquest: that Giles Cory hath several
times afflicted ~~me~~ with several sorts of torments. I veryly think
he is a wizard & afflicted me by witchcraft — Septᵉ 9: 1692
