Mary Warin affirmed to yᵉ Jury of Inquest. That she had
been ~~long~~ afflicted by Giles Cory or his apparition and
that by beating of me with his staffe. & by biting me &
pincking & choking me, greatly tormenting me & cutting me
with a knife. & particularly at yᵉ time of his examination
he did grievously torment me. also at the time of his
examination I saw: D Cory or his apparition most dread-
fully afflict Mary Walcot Ann Putnam mercy Lewis &
Sarah: Vibber Septʳ. 9. 1692

Eliz Woodwell upon yᵉ oath she formerly has taken in
His court: did affirm to yᵉ Jurys of Inquest. that: she saw
Giles Cory at meeting at Salem on a lecture day, since
he has ben at prison he or his apparition: came in &
sat in yᵉ middlemost seat: of yᵉ mens seats: by yᵉ post
this was lecture day before. Bridget Bishup was hanged
and I saw him come out with yᵉ rest of yᵉ people. & then
Mary Walcot affirmed: if saw D Cory. as above. sit in yᵉ
same place at yᵉ same time. he or his appearance & if she
~~did~~ se him go out: with yᵉ rest of yᵉ people. this she affirmed
to yᵉ Jury of Inquest: Septʳ. 9: 1692

Eliz Hubbard: to yᵉ Jury of Inquest that Giles Cory hath several
times afflicted me with several sorts of torments, I verily think
he's a wizard & afflicted me by witchcraft Septʳ 9: 1692
