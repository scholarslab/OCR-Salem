Mary Wason affirmed to y^e Jury of Inquest that she hath beene afflicted by Giles Cory or his apparition and that by beating of me with his staffe and by biting me pinchings e choking me greatly tormenting mee e cutting me with a knife e particularly at y^e time of his examination he did grievously torment me also at the time of his examination I saw: y^e Cory or his apparition most desperately afflicted Mary Walcot Ann Putnam Mary Lewis & Sarah Vibbard Sept. 9: 1692

Eliz Woodwell upon y^e oath she formerly had taken in this court and affrimd to y^e Jury of Inquest that she saw Giles Cory at meeting at Salem on a Lecture day Since he has bin at prison tho or his apparition came in and sat in y^e midst of y^e men’s seats by y^e post his worships were sent before. Bridget Bishop was hanging and saw him come out with y^e rest of y^e people at the same time. Mary Walcot affirmed it. Sarah Cory; & above seven or some place at y^e same time he or his appearance did take out y^e kinge or and with y^e rest of y^e people. this she affirmd to y^e Jury of Inquest. Sept. 9: 1692

Eliz Hubbard to y^e Jury of Inquest that Giles Cory hath several times afflicted her with several sorts of torments, for very few hours he’s a wizard & afflicted me by witchcraft Sept. 9: 1692
