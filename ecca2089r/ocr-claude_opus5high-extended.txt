Mary Warin affirmd: to yᵉ Jury of Jnquest: that: she hath
been: ~~[illegible]~~ afflicted by Giles Cory or his apperisition and
that by beating of me with his staffe: & by: biting mee
pinching & choaking me greatly tortureing me & cutting me
with a knife. & perticulerly at yᵉ time of his examination
he did greivously torment me: also: at the time of his
examination J saw: sᵈ Cory or his appearition most. dredfully afflict: Mary Walcot An putnam: mercy lewis &
Sarah: Vibber Septᵗ 9. 1692

Eliz Woodwell upon yᵉ oath she formerly has taken in
this Court: did affirm to yᵉ Jury of Jnquest: that: she saw
Giles Cory at meeting at Salem on a lecture day: since
he has bin at prison he or his aperartion: came in &
sat: in yᵉ middlemost seat. of yᵉ mens seats: by yᵉ post
this was lecture day before: Bridget Bishup was hanged
and J saw him come out: with yᵉ rest of yᵉ people. ~~at yᵗ time~~
mary Walcot: affirmed: yᵗ Saw sᵈ Cory: as above. sit in yᵉ
same place at yᵉ same time he or his Appearance & yᵗ she
~~[illegible]~~ se him goe out: with yᵉ rest of yᵉ people: this she affirmd
to yᵉ Jury of Jnquest: Septᵗ 9. 1692
