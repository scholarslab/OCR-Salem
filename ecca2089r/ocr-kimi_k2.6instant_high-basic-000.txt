Mary Warin, affirmed: to ye Jury of Inquest: that: she hath
been: ~~often~~ afflicted by Giles Cory or his apparition and
that by beating of me with his staff: & by: biting me
pinching & choaking me greatly torturing me & cutting me
with a knife. & particularly at ye time of his examination
he did grievously torment me: also: at the time of his
examination. I saw: D Cory or his apparition most: dread-
fully afflict: Mary Walcot An Putnam: mercy Lewis &
Sarah: Vibber Sept. 9: 1692

Eliz Woodwell upon ye oath she formerly has taken in
this Court: did affirm to ye Jury of Inquest: that: she saw
Giles Cory at meeting at Salem on a lecture day. Since
he has bin at prison he or his apparition: came in &
sat: in ye middlemost seat: of ye mens seats: by ye post
this was Lecture Day before Bridget Bishop was hanged
and I saw him come out with ye rest of ye people. at ye same
tyme Mary Walcot affirmed: I saw D Cory: as above. sit in ye
same place at ye same tyme he or his appearance & at ye
same tyme goe out: with ye rest of ye people: this she affirmed
to ye Jury of Inquest: Septr 9: 1692

Eliz Hubbard: to ye Jury of Inquest: that Giles Cory hath several
times afflicted ~~both~~ me with several sorts of torments. I verily think
he's a wizard & afflicted me by witchcraft Septr 9: 1692
