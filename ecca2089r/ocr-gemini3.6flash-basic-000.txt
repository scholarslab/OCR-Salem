Mary Warin, affirm'd. to y^e Jury of Inquest. that: she hath
been: ~~[illegible]~~ afflict'd by Giles Cory or his apparition and
that by beating of me with his staffe: & by: biting me
pinching & choaking me greatly tormenting me. & cutting me
with a knife. & perticularly at y^e time of his examina[tion]
he did grevousl^y torment me: also: at the time of his
examination. I saw: s^d Cory or his apparition most dred
fully afflict. Mary Walcot Ann Putnam. mercy lewis &
Sarah: Vibber Sept^r. 9. 1692

Eliz Woodwell upon y^e oath she formerly has taken in
this Court. did affirm to y^e Jury of Inquest: that: she saw
Giles Cory at meeting at Salem on a lecture day. since
he has bin at prison he or his aparition. Came in &
sat: in y^e middlemost seat: of y^e mens seats. by y^e post
this was y^e lecture day before. Bridget Bishop was hanged
and I saw him come out with y^e rest of y^e people. & y^t time
Mary Walcot: affirm'd. y^t I saw s^d Cory: as above. sit in y^e
same place at y^e same time he or his Appearance & y^t she
~~[illegible]~~ se him goe out: with y^e rest of y^e people. this she affirm'd
to y^e Jury of Inquest. sept^r 9: 1692

Eliz Hubbard. to y^e Jury of Inquest. that Giles Cory hath several
times afflict'd ~~me~~ with several sorts of torments. I verily think
he is a wizard & afflict'd me by witchcraft sept^r 9^t: 1692
