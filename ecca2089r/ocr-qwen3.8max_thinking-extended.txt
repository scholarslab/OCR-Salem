Mary Walcot affirm'd to y^e Jury of Inquest that she hath
been ~~sorely~~ afflicted by Giles Cory or his apparition and
that by beating of me with his staffe, & by biting me
pinching & choaking me greatly torturing me, & cutting me
with a knife, & particularly at y^e time of his examination
he did grievously torment me. also: at the time of his
examination, I saw s^d Cory or his apparition most dred
fully afflict. Mary Walcot An Putnam Mercy lewis &
Sarah Vibber Sept^t 9. 1692

Eliz Woodwell upon y^e oath she formerly has taken in
this Court, did affirm to y^e Jury of Inquest that she saw
Giles Cory at meeting at Salem on a lecture day, since
he has been at prison he or his apparition: came in &
sat: in y^e middlemost seat: of y^e mens seats, by y^e post
this was lecture day before: Bridget Bishop was hanged
and I saw him come out, with y^e rest of y^e people ~~at time~~
Mary Walcot: affirmed: y^t she saw s^d Cory: as above, sitting in y^e
same place at y^e same time he or his appearance & y^t she
~~sat~~ she him goe out: with y^e rest of y^e people. this she affirmed
to y^e Jury of Inquest. Sept^t 9: 1692

Eliz Hubbard: to y^e Jury of Inquest that Giles Cory hath several
times afflicted ~~me~~ with several sorts of torments. I verily think
he is a wizard & afflicted me by witchcraft Sept^t 9: 1692
