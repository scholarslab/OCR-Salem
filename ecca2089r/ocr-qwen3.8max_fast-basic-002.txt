Mary Warin affirmed to yᵉ Jury of Inquest that she hath been afflicted by Giles Cory or his apparition and that by beating of me with his staffe, & by biting me pinching & choaking me greatly torturing me, & cutting me with a knife, & particularly at yᵉ time of his examination he did grievously torment me; also at the time of his examination I saw sd Cory or his apparition most dreadfully afflict Mary Walcot An Putnam Mercy Lewis & Sarah Vibber Septʳ 9. 1692

Eliz Woodwell upon yᵉ oath she formerly has taken in this Court, did affirm to yᵉ Jury of Inquest, that she saw Giles Cory at meeting at Salem on a lecture day, since he has bin at prison he or his apparition came in & sat in yᵉ middlemost seat of yᵉ mens seats, by yᵉ post this was yᵉ lecture day before Bridget Bishop was hanged and I saw him come out with yᵉ rest of yᵉ people. Mary Walcot affirmed yᵉ she saw sd Cory as above, sitting in yᵉ same place at yᵉ same time he or his appearance & if she sd he him goe out with yᵉ rest of yᵉ people. This she affirmed to y Jury of Inquest Septʳ 9: 1692

Eliz Hubbard to yᵉ Jury of Inquest that Giles Cory hath several times afflicted me with several sorts of torments. I verily think he is a wizard & afflicted me by witchcraft Septʳ 9: 1692
