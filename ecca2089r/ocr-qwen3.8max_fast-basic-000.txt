Mary Warin affirmd to ye Jury of Inquest that she hath been afflicted by Giles Cory or his apparition and that by beating of me with his staffe, & by biting me pinching & choaking me greatly torturing me, & cutting me with a knife, & particularly at ye time of his examination he did grievously torment me; also at the time of his examination I saw sd Cory or his apparition most dreadfully afflict Mary Walcot An Putnam Mercy lewis & Sarah Vibber Septt. 9. 1692

Eliz Woodwell upon ye oath she formerly has taken in this Court, did affirm to ye Jury of Inquest, that she saw Giles Cory at meeting at Salem on a lecture Day, since he has bin at prison he or his apparition, came in & sat, in ye middlemost seat of ye mens seats, by ye post this was ye lecture Day before Bridget Bishop was hanged and I saw him come out with ye rest of ye people. Mary Walcot affirmed, yt she saw sd Cory, as above, sitting in ye same place at ye same time he or his appearance & yt she saw him goe out with ye rest of ye people. This she affirmd to ye Jury of Inquest. Septt 9: 1692

Eliz Hubbard to ye Jury of Inquest that Giles Cory hath several times afflicted me with several sorts of torments. I verily think he is a wizard & afflicted me by witchcraft Septt 9th 1692
